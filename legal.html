01. Platform Identity & Scope

MyPawTrust (MPT) functions exclusively as a curated, high-integrity marketplace connector. Unless a companion is listed specifically as "Breeder: MPT Florida HQ", we do not own, raise, or directly manage the physical pets. All certified breeders within our network are independent entities but must adhere to the MPT Global Health Protocol.

02. The Security Deposit & Escrow

The $200.00 Reservation Fee is required to secure your specific kitten or puppy. This fee represents the labor cost for vetting records and removing the listing from public availability. All deposits are non-refundable. If the buyer’s circumstances change, the deposit remains valid as a credit toward a future litter within the MPT network for up to 18 months.

03. The MPT Genetic Covenant

We stand by our verified pedigree. Every pet on this site is eligible for a 1-Year Hereditary Health Guarantee. In the event of a verified genetic life-threatening condition (certified by a licensed US veterinarian), MyPawTrust will facilitate a replacement kitten of equal quality. We are not liable for non-genetic/temporary ailments once the pet is in the buyer’s custody beyond 48 hours (standard health check window).
