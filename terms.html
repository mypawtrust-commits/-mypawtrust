Policies & Protections

Ensuring a Safe Journey from Our Home to Yours

🛡️ 1-Year Genetic Health Guarantee

We guarantee that your kitten/puppy is healthy at the time of sale. Each pet is backed by a 1-year health guarantee against congenital or hereditary life-threatening defects.

In the rare event that a hereditary condition is diagnosed by a licensed veterinarian within the first year, MyPawTrust will provide a replacement pet of equal value or offer a partial refund (up to the full purchase price) based on medical evidence.

💰 Reservation & Deposit Policy

A non-refundable deposit of $200.00 is required to reserve a pet. This ensures the pet is removed from the marketplace and held exclusively for you.

Deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred to a future litter if the buyer's circumstances change unexpectedly. The remaining balance must be paid in full at the time of local pickup or 48 hours prior to scheduled flight delivery.

✈️ Shipping & Transit Liability

While we use the most professional Flight Nannies and transport companies, MyPawTrust is not liable for delays or accidents caused by third-party logistics providers or airline carriers.

However, we provide Transit Insurance for all shipped pets. In case of travel-related issues, we will assist you in filing a claim with the insurance provider to ensure full compensation.

⚖️ Terms of Service & Privacy

MyPawTrust acts as a specialized marketplace. We reserve the right to refuse service to any individual we deem unsuitable for pet ownership. We never share your contact information with unauthorized third parties. All transactions processed through our encrypted gateway are monitored for fraud prevention.
